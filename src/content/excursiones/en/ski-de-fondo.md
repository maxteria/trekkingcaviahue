Cross-country skiing is the quietest way to travel a snowy mountain. No lifts, no crowds: only the sound of the skis and the forest.

The proposal includes an introduction for first-timers and traverses for those with some technique. We travel ancient forests, lakes, lagoons and frozen waterfalls.

Josefina teaches cross-country skiing at the Club Andino de Caviahue and works with both beginners and skiers who want to improve.
