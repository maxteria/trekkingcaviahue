Riscos Bayos is a field of volcanic ash formations millions of years old. Wind and rain have carved them into caves, pillars and shapes unlike anything else.

The walk is about 10 km round trip and easy. We take our time, reading the landscape and enjoying the silence.

The place has a special energy: it connects you with calm and a meditative state. That is why we close the trip sharing a picnic at the destination.
