In winter the Agrio waterfalls trail becomes another world: the forest disappears under snow, streams run between white walls and you can actually hear the silence.

We walk on snowshoes, unhurried, stopping at every waterfall. This trip is designed to enjoy the landscape and the snow with no previous experience.

One of the most popular winter excursions in Caviahue.
