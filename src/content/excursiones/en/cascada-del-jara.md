The Jara Waterfall circuit crosses araucaria, lenga and ñire forests along a beautiful trail. After 2 km we reach the waterfall, completely frozen in winter: its ice columns look like the ice age.

It is an easy three-hour trip, ideal for enjoying the mountain without pressure.

Includes poles, snowshoes and a warm tea with a snack to close the walk.
