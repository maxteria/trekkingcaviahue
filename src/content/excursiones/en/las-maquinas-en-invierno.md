Let's go for a "termita"! The traverse to Las Máquinas is one of the most intense and rewarding winter trips.

We walk for several hours through snowy mountains, with slopes and deep snow, until we reach the open-air hot springs. The contrast between the cold trail and the steam of the springs is hard to describe.

It is a demanding trip: you need good fitness and to be ready to walk long in snow conditions.
