The Hidden Lagoon is the roof of Caviahue. We climb on snowshoes along a trail of snow and araucarias until we reach the lagoon, where the village appears below.

The ascent is steady but accessible. At the top a landscape that will make you sigh: snow, forest and silence.

One of the best ways to understand the geography of Caviahue.
