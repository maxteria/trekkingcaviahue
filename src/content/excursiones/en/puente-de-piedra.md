The Puente de Piedra excursion is an easy trek, suitable for almost anyone.

We leave Caviahue by vehicle, drive 6 km along the road and walk into the trail towards the shore of Lake Caviahue. After 2 km this wonderful formation appears, on the southern arm of the lake, shaped 10,000 years ago by glacial erosion.

From there you can see different formations of the ancient volcanic caldera lake and the majestic Copahue Volcano. A trail you cannot miss if you come to Caviahue.

Includes poles and a snack at the destination.
