The climb to the active crater of Copahue Volcano is Caviahue's signature excursion: you walk to the rim of the crater at 2,850 m, across a landscape of volcanic ash, sulphur and steam.

## Two ways up

- **Classic option:** start at Las Mellizas lagoon (1,950 m), 11 km round trip and almost 800 metres of elevation gain.
- **4x4 + trekking option:** the vehicle drops us at 2,300 m and the challenge begins on the "El Caracol" trail.

## How the day unfolds

We leave at 07:00 and are back around 14:00. I pick you up at your accommodation in Caviahue or Copahue.

The terrain combines ash trails, sustained slopes and a final scramble over volcanic scree. It is demanding, but every metre pays off: the active crater, the border marker with Chile and a view you will not forget.

Suitable from age 12, always with good fitness and some experience of long walks.
