Cerro Pirámide is my favourite in Caviahue by far. It is beautiful the whole way, and at the summit it feels like another planet, straight out of a movie.

It is the best place for panoramic views of Caviahue and the whole surrounding range, with many well-known volcanoes and peaks in Chile and Argentina.

## The walk

14 km, 849 metres of elevation gain and between 6 and 7 hours on foot. You climb from 1,620 m to 2,498 m.

It is a demanding trek, moderate to hard: come with good fitness and the will to walk long.
