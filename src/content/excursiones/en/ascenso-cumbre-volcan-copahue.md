The summit of Copahue Volcano is the roof of this part of the Andes: 2,958 m, right beside the border marker with Chile.

It is a longer and harder day than the crater trek. We walk calmly, managing our energy, over loose volcanic ground with the wind as a companion.

At the top the reward is complete: the crater at your feet, the crater lagoon, the Neuquén steppe on one side and Chile on the other.
