The Hidden Waterfall is Caviahue's zen corner. The trail feels like a fairy tale: snow, ancient araucarias and a silence you do not find anywhere else.

We walk on snowshoes to the waterfall. Arriving is disconnection, beauty and peace.

It is a short, accessible trip, ideal for first-time snowshoers or for going with children.
